Honeytongue / Phaser example

The bridge troll

A small Phaser game with a Honeytongue character. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or tap where to go. Press E, Space, or Enter to read the sign or talk to the troll.

The whole example is about 250 lines: read the source, or see Visual games in the docs.

Made with Honeytongue and Phaser. Jev judges what you say, and the game decides what happens. Sprites by Tristan Broughton.
